Are you ready for Global Layer One?

Global Layer One (GL1) is the Monetary Authority of Singapore's initiative to build shared, multi-purpose ledger infrastructure for tokenised financial assets. Check off each action item your organisation has completed, then get your readiness score.

GL1 is an evolving industry initiative, not a finalised rulebook. Its common principles, policies and standards are still being developed (including through a proposed non-profit GL1 Org). This checklist covers the regulatory, security and governance foundations GL1 participants should expect to meet. It is not legal advice.
